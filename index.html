I'm a student, currently seeking full- or part-time employment in a creative and interesting role.

Following my long and arduous years of employment in many facets of the IT industry, I have bravely decided to attempt a dramatic career pivot. I recently completed two years of study in graphic and web design, and have now branched out into creative marketing.

Creativity is where I experience great joy and believe that I have finally found my path forward. I read voraciously, visits art galleries and exhibitions, make both digital and traditional art, and create and perform music. I'm also really into table-top roleplaying games and am currently running a D&D campaign. I enjoy computer gaming as well, when I has the time.

My dream job, asides from being a designer, is to work at either an art gallery, book store, library, print shop, or museum.
